About

Hello! My name is Christian, and I'm a Computer Science student at California State University, San Bernardino focused on software engineering and applied AI. I learn by building: my projects range from The Archive, a full-stack web app where users share and discuss historical media, to VibeCheck, an AI-powered music recommendation app built with retrieval-augmented generation. I've also contributed to open source, with a merged pull request in PathReview. I'm always looking for opportunities to build useful things, sharpen my skills, and grow as a developer. Outside of coding, I enjoy music and challenging myself with new ideas.
