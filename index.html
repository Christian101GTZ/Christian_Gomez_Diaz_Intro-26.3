About

I'm Christian, a Computer Science student at California State University, San Bernardino graduating in May 2027. I learn by building. My work ranges from The Archive, a deployed full-stack platform where users publish, vote on, and discuss media worth preserving, to VibeCheck, a hybrid AI music recommender that pairs Gemini with a deterministic scoring engine, to a retrieval-augmented game discovery system built on Sentence Transformers and ChromaDB.

Beyond personal projects, I've contributed to PathReview, an open-source portfolio review tool, worked as an undergraduate researcher in CSUSB's AI Research Lab, and earned honors in all four CodePath courses I've completed, from intro web development through Applications of AI Engineering. I'm bilingual in English and Spanish and looking for new-grad and early-career software engineering and AI engineering roles. Outside of coding, I enjoy music and challenging myself with new ideas.
